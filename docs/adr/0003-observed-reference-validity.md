# Require observed reference points for initialization and evaluation

The original reader accepted C3D residuals greater than or equal to zero. The C3D format assigns zero residuals to processed coordinates, including interpolation, filtering and generated points. An audit of 646 readable training/validation C3Ds found exact agreement between zero residuals and missing camera contributions. Leading processed coordinates in several OS/PS recordings contain deterministic linear position and heading extrapolations. They supplied incorrect initial headings despite passing the old distance/rigidity checks.

The amended reader requires positive residuals. When an export supplies populated camera masks, both required markers must also have camera contributions. Camera masks are optional in C3D: absent or globally unpopulated masks alone do not prove a coordinate invalid. Filtering and differentiation operate within contiguous observed segments; initial pose comes from the first observed synchronized frame. Reference gaps remain masked and never reset reconstruction. A recording without sufficient observed labels is excluded by the existing duration gate, with its reason recorded.

Preserve earlier sources and results. Compare old and corrected reconstructions on identical observed frames and recordings, while disclosing that initialization and reference coverage changed. Do not describe the resulting reduction as a neural-model improvement on the old full-duration benchmark. The v7 isolation experiment inherits fixed v5 clock mappings and neural weights; further clock/model fitting must use observed training labels. Participant assignments stay unchanged and test signals stay unopened during development.
